Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 1, 2025

Tweakly ("we," "us," or "our") is a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform that helps users optimize resumes, generate tailored cover letters, and plan professional outreach. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store and protect it, and what rights you have regarding your data.

By creating an account or using Tweakly, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information: Email address, first name, last name, and Google profile data if you sign in with Google.

1.2 Authentication Credentials: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a unique salt. We never store plaintext passwords.

1.3 Resume and Document Content: Content from DOCX uploads or Google Docs you select for processing.

1.4 Job Description Content: Job description text you paste into the platform.

1.5 Generated Content: Optimized resumes, cover letters, and outreach messages you generate.

1.6 Usage and Analytics Data: Basic aggregate usage data (pages visited, features used, session duration).

1.7 Cookies and Local Storage: Essential cookies and local storage to maintain sessions and preferences.

2. Google OAuth and Google Docs Access

2.1 Google Sign-In: We use Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate and access basic profile info.

2.2 Google Docs Read and Write Access: We request the documents scope to read and write only the docs you explicitly select.

2.3 Token Storage and Revocation: Tokens are stored securely; you can revoke access at any time via Google Account permissions.

2.4 Compliance: We comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Service Delivery: Provide resume optimization, cover letters, outreach, and exports.

3.2 Account Management: Manage your account and communicate service updates.

3.3 Service Improvement: Use aggregated data to improve product experience.

3.4 Security and Fraud Prevention: Protect platform integrity.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.2+ for all traffic.

4.2 Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data stored with encryption at rest.

4.3 Password Security: bcrypt with unique salts.

4.4 Infrastructure: Cloud hosting with backups and network isolation.

4.5 Access Controls: Least privilege access to production systems.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

5.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data: We do not sell personal information or resume content.

5.2 Service Providers: Limited sharing with trusted vendors for hosting, email, analytics.

5.3 AI Processing: We may use third-party AI APIs under data processing agreements.

5.4 Legal Requirements: We may disclose if required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

6.1 Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active.

6.2 Account Deletion: You may request deletion at tweaklyai@gmail.com. We delete within 30 days, with backups purged within 90 days.

6.3 Temporary Processing Data: One-time processing data is retained only for the session.

7. Your Rights

Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, and applicable rights under CCPA and GDPR. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Tweakly is not intended for users under 13 (or 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States. By using the service, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy and will post updates on this page.
